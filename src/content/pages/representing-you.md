  - type: markdown
    heading: Your Voice in Primary Care
    body: |
      Manchester LMC has a statutory right to be consulted by NHS England and Greater Manchester ICB on all matters affecting general practice. We attend commissioning meetings, workforce forums, and primary care strategy groups to make sure that the GP perspective is heard and respected.

      Our elected committee members bring intelligence from across Manchester — from Cheetham Hill to Wythenshawe — to ensure that representation reflects the full diversity of our member practices.
