Salaried GP at Urban Village Medical Practice, Ancoats. An LMC member since September 2012, Samira served as GP Registrar representative from 2012 to 2016, has been an executive member since 2018, and was Vice Chair from December 2023 to December 2025 before becoming Chair in January 2026. She leads LMC representation at ICB and NHS England forums and chairs the quarterly partners meeting.
