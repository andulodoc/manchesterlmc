# Project Context: Manchester LMC website

## Overview
This project is the official website for the **Manchester Local Medical Committee (LMC)**. The LMC is the statutory body that represents, advises, and supports General Practitioners (GPs) and practice staff across the city of Manchester.

## Purpose
The website serves as a central hub for:
- **Representation:** Communicating the LMC’s work in negotiating and advocating for Manchester GPs.
- **Guidance:** Providing up-to-date professional advice on GP contracts, HR, and clinical governance.
- **Communication:** Sharing news, newsletters, and alerts relevant to primary care.
- **Support:** Directing healthcare professionals to wellbeing resources and training events.

## Target Audience
- General Practitioners (Partners, Salaried, and Locums).
- Practice Managers and administrative staff.

## Technical Structure
- **Environment:** Hosted via GitHub Pages.
- **Nature:** A content-driven static site focused on accessibility and clear information architecture.
- **Key Content Areas:** News/Updates, Guidance library, Committee member profiles, and Event listings.

## Areas to develop
- Implementing Eleventy for easy news updates
- A functional, secure, members login area to add/remove/update documents, add news/events
- Breach reporting form to report breaches of hospital contract and inappropriate workload transfer
